Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: Askbox collects nothing about you. The messages it reads stay on your iPhone, and Askbox has no way to send them anywhere.

What Askbox reads

Askbox only sees the messages a Shortcuts automation you set up passes to it: the sender, the conversation, the text and which app it came from (WhatsApp, Telegram or Slack). It can't read your chats or anything you haven't set the automation up for, and it drops messages from apps you've turned off in its settings as soon as they arrive.

Where it's read

Askbox decides whether a message asks you to do something on your iPhone, with Apple Intelligence when it's on and simpler built-in rules when it isn't. The message isn't sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else. Askbox contains no network code, no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code.

What's kept, and for how long

Each suggestion is stored on your iPhone, inside Askbox, protected by your passcode. It keeps the task, the date and who asked. The message itself is deleted after 7 days, or sooner if you choose 1 Day or Until I Decide in Settings › Privacy & Data. Suggestions you've added or dismissed are deleted after 90 days. Delete All Data in the same place removes everything at once, and deleting the app does too.

Reminders

iOS only offers full access to Reminders, so that's what Askbox asks for. It reads the names of your lists so you can pick one, adds the reminders you approve, and deletes one only if you undo it from History. It doesn't read your other reminders. If Include Message in Notes is on, the message is copied into the reminder's notes; Reminders then keeps it like any other reminder, including in iCloud if you sync Reminders.

Notifications

Askbox's notifications are created on your iPhone and show the task and the message it came from, like any notification on your lock screen. Nothing passes through a server.

Contacting support

Contact Support in Settings opens an email in your own mail app. Askbox adds the app version, iOS version and iPhone model to it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. They don't include your messages or reminders.

Children

Askbox collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
