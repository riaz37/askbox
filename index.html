How it works

A Shortcuts automation you turn on passes each new message to Askbox. Askbox decides on your iPhone whether it asks something of you, with Apple Intelligence when it's on and simpler built-in rules when it isn't. If it does, you get one notification with the task and when it's due.

Check it, then add it

Every ask keeps the message it came from, so a wrong guess is easy to spot. Swipe right to add it to Reminders, left to skip it, or tap to change the day first.

Privacy

Everything happens on your iPhone. Askbox has no account, no servers, no analytics and no network code. Messages are kept 7 days by default, or less if you choose.

What you need

An iPhone with iOS 27, and the Shortcuts and Reminders apps. Works best with Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or later); without it, Askbox uses simpler rules and may miss more asks.
