Common questions

Why does Askbox need a shortcut?

iOS doesn't let one app read another app's notifications. A Shortcuts automation you turn on is the only way to pass a message to Askbox. To go through the steps again, open Settings › Set Up Again in Askbox.

Askbox says it hasn't seen any messages yet.

Open Shortcuts › Automation and check there's a Notification automation for each chat app, set to Run Immediately, with the action Suggest Reminder From Message. Settings › Status in Askbox shows when each app last passed a message.

Some messages never show up.

Messages silenced by a Focus, apps with notification previews turned off, and notifications held for a scheduled summary may not reach the shortcut, or reach it late. In Low Power Mode, iOS may delay Apple Intelligence; those messages wait in Asks until you open Askbox.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 27. It works best with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, turned on in Settings). Without it, or while the model is still downloading, Askbox runs in Basic Mode: simpler rules, also on your iPhone, that may miss more asks. Askbox tells you which mode it's in.

It suggested something that wasn't an ask.

Swipe it left to skip it. If it happens often, keep Settings › Sensitivity on Only Clear Asks. You can also tap any ask to change it before adding it.

I added a reminder by mistake.

Tap Undo right away, or find it in History and undo it there. That deletes the reminder from Reminders.

Why does it ask for full access to Reminders?

iOS doesn't offer anything smaller. Askbox only adds the reminders you approve, and never reads your other reminders.

How do I delete what Askbox keeps?

Settings › Privacy & Data › Delete All Data. Reminders you already added stay in Reminders. See the Privacy Policy for what's kept and for how long.
